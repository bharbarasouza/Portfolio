About

I like turning messy processes into systems people can rely on.

I've spent seven years at Toptal on the operations side of a global talent network: screening, onboarding, and supporting talent after activation. The part of the work I like most is taking a manual, messy process and turning it into something tracked, automated, and measurable.

Along the way I've led a team of 13+ screeners, designed a 4-week onboarding program, run cross-team projects from documentation overhauls to training workshops, and built pipeline trackers with reporting dashboards and automated follow-up workflows.

I do my best work where operations meets data and delivery. I'm a Professional Scrum Master I finishing a bachelor's in Computer Science, and I work in English, Portuguese, and Spanish.
