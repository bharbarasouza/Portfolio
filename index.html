About

The part of the work I like most is taking a manual, messy process and turning it into something people can track, trust and measure.

My background is in people and operations. I started in crew relations on cruise ships, moved into interviewing candidates, led a team of 13 screeners, and now support new talent through onboarding and their first months.

Along the way I've designed a four-week onboarding program, run projects with other teams, from documentation overhauls to training workshops, and built trackers with reporting dashboards and automated follow-ups.

I do my best work where operations meets data and delivery. I'm finishing a bachelor's in Computer Science.

Selected work

Six projects, each one a manual process made easier to run.

  1. Certification tracking workflow

    Current roleMade with Google Forms, Apps Script, Gmail and Slack

    A company certification initiative depended on manual follow-up to know who had enrolled, booked and passed. I took it over and rebuilt it with a form intake and status tracking, daily notifications, digests of overdue exams, weekly follow-up batches and a library of outreach templates.

  2. Talent pipeline tracker

    Current roleMade with Google Sheets and Apps Script

    Talent moving through onboarding and the months after was tracked by hand, so status was hard to see and easy to lose. I built a tracker that moves records between stages automatically, clears expired ones every day and reports on the whole pipeline in a dashboard.

  3. Keeping a team busy through a slowdown

    2023With the Publications team

    When call volume for the screening team dropped, a team of 13 had capacity to spare. I set up a partnership with Publications so screeners took on SEO cross-referencing, blog comment follow-ups and article review.

  4. Skills reference and script trainer

    2023Made with Google Sheets and Google Sites

    Screeners relied on an outdated skills spreadsheet, and new interview scripts were longer and harder to learn. With the Technical Screeners, I rebuilt the spreadsheet into a reference organized by industry, and built an interactive tool for practicing the new scripts.

  5. Documentation overhauls

    2022 to 2023Made in Confluence

    Documentation for the screening team and for Talent Acquisition had grown outdated and hard to navigate. I led two overhauls with the screeners, restructuring both spaces so anyone across the teams could find what they needed.

  6. Four-week onboarding program

    2021Training materials and process documentation

    New Communications Specialists had no structured way to get up to speed. I designed and launched a four-week program for the whole team, with training materials, written processes and regular feedback from the people going through it.
